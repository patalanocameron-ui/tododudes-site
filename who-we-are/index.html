Empowering the next generation through meaningful work.

Since starting on May 14, 2020, The To-Do Dudes has completed over 12,000 projects for more than 4,000 customers.

flagOur mission

Good work that builds good people.

We support our team members as they jump-start their careers or build on their experience by serving our community.

We strive to create a positive work environment with growth opportunities for everyone, now and in the future. Every job our neighbors book helps a young person in Flagler and Volusia County build real skills.
