We cancel due to weather. If we determine conditions are unsafe before work begins, we will reschedule at no charge.

We arrive but can't safely start. A 1-hour minimum charge applies (this replaces the 2-hour minimum). We will reschedule the rest at no additional fee.

Weather delays during a job. If we pause for weather after work has started, billing pauses. When we resume, billing resumes (in 15-minute increments per Section 4.2). You are charged for time actually worked, subject to the standard 2-hour minimum.

Customer cancellation for weather. Standard cancellation terms in Section 4.5 apply to all cancellations, regardless of the customer's weather concerns. We do not waive cancellation fees for weather conditions we determine are safe to work in.
