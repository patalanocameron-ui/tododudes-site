helpFAQ

Good questions.

What do the To-Do Dudes do?expand_more

The To-Do Dudes is a home labor team based in Palm Coast, Florida. We send background-checked, trained team members to help with yard work, heavy lifting, furniture moving, garage and attic clean outs, assembly, event setup and other odd jobs around the house. You book by the hour and choose how many Dudes you need.

How much does it cost?expand_more

Visits are booked in time blocks with two Dudes. A 2-hour Quick Fix Visit is $236, a 4-hour Half-Day Help is $472 and an 8-hour Full Day Project is $944. Chore Club members pay $196, $392 and $784. Custom bookings are available.

Do you bring a truck or mow lawns?expand_more

No. We are a labor-only service, so we don't drive trucks or supply vehicles, and we don't do chemical lawn treatment or mowing with our own equipment. We provide the hands and the hustle. You provide the truck, tools or POD when a job needs one.

What areas do you serve?expand_more

We serve Palm Coast, Flagler Beach, Ormond Beach, Daytona Beach and Port Orange. We can also visit nearby areas like St. Augustine for an out-of-area fee of $25 per Dude.

What is the on-time guarantee?expand_more

If your Dudes arrive even one minute late, you get $10 off per Dude.

Are the Dudes insured and background checked?expand_more

Yes. Every Dude goes through an application, screening call, interview, in-person onboarding and supervised field training before working on their own. The company carries liability coverage and workers compensation insurance, and all team members are 18 or older.

How soon can I get help?expand_more

Most jobs can be scheduled within the same week. Book online or call (386) 597-0722 to pick your day, time and number of Dudes.
